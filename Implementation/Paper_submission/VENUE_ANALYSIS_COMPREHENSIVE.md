## ANTICIPATED REVIEWER CONCERNS & RESPONSES

### **Concern 1: "Sample period too short (2020-2025)"**

**Response:**
- "We acknowledge this limitation in Section 5.4. The 6-year period includes diverse market regimes (COVID crash 2020, recovery 2021, inflation volatility 2022, AI boom 2023, regime change 2025), providing robust regime coverage. Out-of-sample validation on 2026+ data is ongoing and will be reported in future work."

### **Concern 2: "Small universe (27-35 tickers)"**

**Response:**
- "Our focus on liquid blue-chips (Nifty 50/100) ensures realistic execution assumptions and avoids survivorship bias. Academic studies using 100-500 stocks often include illiquid names with unrealistic transaction cost assumptions. Section 5.5.1 proposes Nifty 500 / BSE 500 extension as immediate future work."

### **Concern 3: "ML non-determinism (Section 3.6.7)"**

**Response:**
- "We transparently report this limitation and its impact (Run 1 vs Run 2 comparison). The non-determinism arises from TensorFlow GPU parallelism despite seed=42. Importantly, the AGGREGATE results (mean Sharpe over 6 folds) remain stable, and the statistical significance conclusion (p=0.32) is robust. Section 5.5.1 proposes deterministic CPU-only configuration for publication reproducibility."

### **Concern 4: "India result is data snooping (tested after NSE failed)"**

**Response:**
- "We acknowledge this concern in Section 5.4.2. However, India was selected based on a priori market structure hypotheses (retail dominance 45%, momentum clustering literature, sector concentration), not post-hoc cherry-picking. The 16x magnitude (vs marginal NSE improvement) suggests structural advantage, not random luck. Section 5.5.1 proposes pre-registered replication on 2026+ data to confirm persistence."

### **Concern 5: "Rolling window improvement is not statistically significant (p=0.32)"**

**Response:**
- "We explicitly report this in Section 3.6.5 and do NOT claim statistical significance. The +113% improvement (+0.461 Sharpe) is economically meaningful (73% trade reduction, 89% cost drag elimination) but lacks statistical power (n=6 folds, high variance). This HONESTY strengthens our geographic alpha conclusion: even optimized methodology (+0.052) is dwarfed by multi-market India (+0.840, 16x better)."
