### **Concern 1: "Sample period too short (2020-2025)"**

**Response:**
> "We acknowledge this limitation in Section 5.4. The 6-year period includes diverse market regimes: COVID crash (2020), recovery (2021), inflation volatility (2022), AI boom (2023), and regime change (2025). Out-of-sample validation on 2026+ data is ongoing."

---

### **Concern 2: "Small universe (27-35 tickers)"**

**Response:**
> "Our focus on liquid blue-chips (Nifty 50/100) ensures realistic execution assumptions. Academic studies with 100-500 stocks often include illiquid names with unrealistic transaction cost assumptions. Section 5.5.1 proposes Nifty 500 extension as immediate future work."

---

### **Concern 3: "ML non-determinism (Section 3.6.7)"**

**Response:**
> "We transparently report this limitation (Run 1 vs Run 2 comparison, TensorFlow GPU randomness despite seed=42). Importantly, AGGREGATE results (mean Sharpe over 6 folds) remain stable, and the statistical significance conclusion (p=0.32) is robust. Section 5.5.1 proposes deterministic CPU-only config for reproducibility."

---

### **Concern 4: "India result is data snooping (tested after NSE failed)"**

**Response:**
> "We acknowledge this concern in Section 5.4.2. However, India was selected based on a priori market structure hypotheses: retail dominance (45% volume), momentum clustering (literature support), sector concentration (40% financials). The 16x magnitude suggests structural advantage, not random luck. Section 5.5.1 proposes pre-registered 2026+ replication."

---

### **Concern 5: "Rolling window improvement is not statistically significant (p=0.32)"**

**Response:**
> "We EXPLICITLY report this in Section 3.6.5 and do NOT claim statistical significance. The +113% improvement is economically meaningful (73% trade reduction, 89% cost drag elimination) but lacks statistical power (n=6, high variance). This HONESTY strengthens our geographic alpha conclusion: even optimized methodology (+0.052) is dwarfed by multi-market India (+0.840, 16x better)."
